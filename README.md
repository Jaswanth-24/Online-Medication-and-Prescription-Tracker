# 💊 MedCare – Online Medication & Prescription Tracking System

MedCare is a web-based healthcare management platform developed during my internship to streamline medication tracking and prescription management. It enables patients, doctors, and healthcare organizations to interact within a unified system for better medical coordination and efficiency.

The platform allows users to manage their medications, follow prescribed treatments, and maintain organized health records. Doctors can prescribe medications, monitor patient progress, and access treatment history, while healthcare organizations can digitally manage and provide their services through the system.

MedCare is designed with a focus on usability, security, and scalability, making it a practical solution for modern digital healthcare needs.
